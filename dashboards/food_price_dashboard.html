What a retailer or distributor should take from this

  • Watch edible oil. Palm oil is up 18% on 2022 in the same markets; its year-over-year change peaked at +25.2% (Jan 2025) and stood at +9.1% in Jul 2026.
  • Wheat flour has swung from deflation to inflation. -17.2% year over year in May 2024, +8.6% in Jul 2026: renegotiate supply terms while it is rising.
  • Lentils and coarse rice are cheaper than a year ago (-9.7% and -8.7% in their latest well-covered months): a window to build stock.
  • Geography matters less than timing for staples. Where at least 3 markets report, divisional prices sit within about ±8% of the national average; the risk is in price swings over time, not in where you buy.
