01 / PROBLEM & SOLUTION

From market records to clearer decisions

Retail buyers need to know which commodities are moving unusually, without confusing measurement units, regions or time periods. This project implements an auditable data pipeline and monitoring dashboard that separates descriptive price signals from business claims.

Scope safeguard: The latest source observation is September 2026, but May 2026 is the last month meeting the project's comparison-coverage heuristic. The strict risk rankings describe four markets in the Chittagong area—not Bangladesh as a whole.

03 / ENGINEERING

How the pipeline works

01

Ingest & profile

Load the user-downloaded WFP release, inspect units, source flags, history and completeness.

02

Clean & validate

Repair labels, quarantine invalid dates, retain possible real-world outliers with flags.

03

Model & analyze

Build SQLite/PostgreSQL star tables, matched-market MoM/YoY analyses and 12-month fixed market risk scores.

04

Test & publish

Recalculate indicators independently and build the dashboard, figures, case study and report.

Design principle: compare the same market IDs at both endpoints; for the risk score compare the identical panel in all twelve months. Keep BDT/kg, BDT/litre and BDT/piece separate.
04 / BUSINESS RESPONSE

Where the insights help

Supplier quote reviews

Start with the high-scoring local commodities. Confirm pricing changes against quotes, quality, transport costs and seasonality before ordering.

Purchasing cadence

Watch repeated matched-market increases and volatility. More frequent checks may be justified, but this dataset cannot verify savings or predict future prices.

Evidence quality

Disclose geographic sample sizes, actual versus aggregate source flags, and missing comparisons before making operational recommendations.

Refresh discipline

Download a fresh release, run the pipeline, rerun validation, and rebuild the dashboard with the newest eligible comparison month.

05 / SOURCE & LIMITATIONS

Responsible interpretation

This independent portfolio analysis uses original World Food Programme Bangladesh food-price observations obtained from the Humanitarian Data Exchange. It is not official CPI, a national inflation forecast, or a measured business improvement. Redistribution of source materials depends on verifying the license for the exact WFP/HDX release.
